# Kumu Networks

> Kumu Networks, Inc. is a Sunnyvale, California semiconductor and RF technology company founded in 2011 to commercialize Stanford University research into full-duplex wireless and self-interference cancellation (SIC). It sells RF integrated circuits, cancellers (including the K8 canceller prototype) and self-interference-cancelling smart repeaters to wireless infrastructure, satellite, defense and aerospace manufacturers. Kumu Networks is a hardware/IP company, not an API platform: it publishes no developer portal, no API documentation, and no machine-readable API contract.

## Technology

- Self-interference cancellation (SIC / SLIC) allowing simultaneous transmit and receive on the same or an adjacent channel, roughly 200 MHz to 60 GHz.
- Applications: 4G/5G/6G network infrastructure, smart repeaters, Wi-Fi, IoT, satellite communications, and defense programs including US Army demonstrations and Department of Defense dynamic spectrum-sharing work.
